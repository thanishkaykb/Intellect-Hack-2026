# Intellect Hack 2026

Build a complete, production-quality, highly polished event website for INTELLECT HACK 2026, an AI Hackathon / Competition organized by IEEE Reliability Society, SEC SBC, hosted at Sri Sairam Engineering College, Chennai.

This is an official college-level inter-college event. The website must feel like a premium technology competition website, NOT like a generic college event page.

CORE VISUAL DIRECTION

Theme:

Dark, premium, classic technology aesthetic

Primary palette: deep maroon / wine red / crimson + black

Background: near-black with subtle maroon gradients

Accent: elegant red/maroon glow

Use white/off-white typography for readability

Avoid excessive bright colors

Avoid childish neon cyberpunk styling

Overall feeling: prestigious, mysterious, technical, modern and memorable

Typography:

Use a sophisticated modern display font for headings

Use a highly readable modern sans-serif for body text

Heading typography should feel premium and editorial

Strong hierarchy between event title, section headings and body text

Design language:

Premium hackathon landing page

Smooth animations

Subtle glassmorphism where appropriate

Fine borders

Soft shadows

Maroon glow effects

Grain/noise texture in the background

Subtle animated particles or abstract technical background elements

Do NOT overuse animations

Everything should remain professional and readable

The website must be fully responsive for:

Desktop

Laptop

Tablet

Mobile

Use smooth scrolling and polished micro-interactions throughout the website.

FAQ SECTION

Create an interactive accordion FAQ section.

Include these questions and answers:

Who can participate?

Students from 2nd and 3rd year departments can participate. The event is an inter-college competition.

How many members can be in a team?

Each team must have 2–4 members.

What is the registration fee?

The registration fee is ₹200 per team.

Is there an online preliminary round?

Yes. The online preliminary round begins on 11 September 2026.

What do we submit in the online round?

Teams must submit a PowerPoint presentation explaining their problem understanding, proposed solution, methodology, feasibility and expected outcomes.

Where will the online problem statements be provided?

The five predefined problem statements will be circulated through a Google Form.

Can we choose any problem statement?

Yes. Each team may choose any ONE of the five predefined problem statements.

When is the submission deadline?

The PowerPoint submission deadline is 19 September 2026.

When will shortlisted teams be announced?

Shortlisted teams will be announced before or on 23 September 2026, with communication sent through email.

When is the offline round?

The offline grand finale takes place on 25 September 2026.

Where is the offline event?

The offline event will be conducted at Apple Hall, Sri Sairam Engineering College, Chennai.

Will the offline problem statement be given beforehand?

No. A new problem statement will be announced on the spot at the venue.

How long is the offline event?

The offline event duration is 6 hours.

What happens during the offline round?

The first half focuses on understanding the problem, preparing a solution concept and presenting it. The second half focuses on developing/refining the prototype and presenting a working demonstration followed by a Q&A session.

How will the winners be selected?

Winners will be selected based on the overall evaluation of the PowerPoint presentation and prototype demonstration.
