## 🏆 Competition Structure

### Phase I — Online Preliminary Round

The preliminary round allows participating teams to select one of the predefined problem statements and present their proposed AI-based solution.

Teams are expected to demonstrate:

- Problem understanding
- Proposed solution
- Methodology
- Feasibility
- Expected outcomes

Teams submit their PowerPoint presentation through the official submission process.

### Phase II — Offline Grand Finale

Shortlisted teams participate in the offline finale at Sri Sairam Engineering College.

A new problem statement is announced at the venue and is not disclosed beforehand.

The finale consists of two major stages:

#### 💡 Ideate & Present

Teams:

1. Understand the newly announced challenge
2. Develop a solution concept
3. Prepare their presentation
4. Present their approach to the jury

#### 🛠️ Build & Demonstrate

Teams then:

1. Develop or refine their prototype
2. Demonstrate the working solution
3. Present the prototype
4. Participate in a technical Q&A session

Final results are based on the overall evaluation of the presentation and prototype demonstration.
